# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.
The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Expenses: record money the business has already paid out that isn't a purchase invoice (rent, fuel, bank fees). Find them under Purchases → Expenses in the sidebar, with their own list and form. Pick the account it was paid from (Cash, Bank, or a liability with no account type, such as a loan) and add one or more rows, each with an Expense account and an amount. (see spec 0004)
- Submitting an expense posts it to the ledger: each row debits its account, and the total credits the account it was paid from. It then shows in the General Ledger (with "Expenses" as a Ref Type), Top Expenses, and profit and loss for the month of its date. Cancelling reverses those rows. To correct a submitted expense, cancel it and use Duplicate.
- New expenses are numbered from the `EXP-` series by default (Books Defaults → Expense Number Series). Existing sites get the series through a patch when they run `bench migrate`.
- A warning when an expense repeats a supplier's reference number already used on a submitted expense you can read. The save still goes through.
- Expenses on the phone: an "Expense" entry in the dashboard's create menu, a two line list row, and rows edited in a sheet.
- Expenses in search (by number, supplier or reference number), in the Linked entries of a supplier or account (with the expense total), in the Import Wizard (saved as drafts), and in a new "Business - Expense" print format, which is the default.
- A Books User can record expenses without rights on the chart of accounts. Paid From and the row accounts are picked from two server searches that offer only the accounts a save accepts. A Books Cashier gets no access to expenses.
- An Account balances card on the dashboard lists each cash and bank account with its balance as of today, and a Total. A positive balance shows in green, a negative one in red with a minus sign. Click an account to open the General Ledger for it up to that date. The card ignores the period picker, and it scrolls with the header and Total kept in view. Users who can't read the ledger and the chart of accounts (Books User, Books Cashier) don't see it. (see spec 0001)
- Choose which accounts the dashboard's Account balances card lists: Settings → General has a new Dashboard section with a Dashboard Accounts table. Pick any leaf Asset or Liability account, such as petty cash or a loan; a liability shows its balance in red and lowers the Total. With the table empty, the card lists every Cash and Bank account, as before. (see spec 0003)
- A "Choose accounts" button on the Account balances card opens those settings, for users who can change Accounting Settings.
- A 30 day trial for hosted sites: days 1 to 21 are the free trial, and from day 22 a banner on every /books screen counts down the days left to subscribe. System Managers and Books Managers get a Subscribe button; everyone else is told to ask their administrator. You can dismiss the banner until the next day. Days follow the time zone in System Settings (UTC while it is empty). A site is hosted only when its `site_config.json` has `books_control_url`; a self hosted site has no clock, no banner and no lock, and works as before. (see spec 0005)
- From day 26 without a subscription, a hosted site turns read only. The server refuses every create, edit, submit, cancel and delete, for every role including Administrator, and /books shows a "Read only" notice with the banner's text instead of an error. Viewing, lists, reports, search, print, PDF download, CSV export, login, logout and password reset keep working. Queued background jobs write nothing while the site is locked. The banner turns red, can't be dismissed, and names the day 30 date to subscribe by.
- A Subscription page in the sidebar shows your trial dates or your paid dates and the plans offered for your company's country: ClickPesa yearly in Tanzania, PayPal monthly or yearly everywhere else. Subscribe opens the payment page. While a payment is being confirmed, the lock notice says so for up to 24 hours. Coming back from checkout fetches the new state, so the lock and the banner clear without a reload. When billing can't be reached, the page says "Couldn't reach billing. Try again." with a Retry button, and your access is unchanged.
- Paid sites: a site that doesn't renew on its own gets a renew banner 14 days before its paid date ends, then 7 days of grace with full access, then turns read only with "Renew to make changes again." Paying lifts the lock on the very next request.
- Books Subscription holds the trial and payment facts. No user can change it, Administrator included: it changes only through the signed updates from the billing control site, the install and patch code that starts the trial, and `bench execute frappe_books.subscription.apply_state`. Every change is kept as a Version. Existing sites start their trial when they run `bench migrate`. Taking payments in production waits on the billing control site, which is not built yet.
- `frappe_books.subscription.get_subscription` takes `with_plans` (true by default). With `with_plans=False` it returns the status alone, with no `plans` or `plans_error`, and never calls the billing control site, whatever `refresh` says. /books uses it before it words a lock notice. (see spec 0007, lock hardening)

### Changed
- A message the server sends with a successful save, delete, submit or cancel now shows as a toast on every form, instead of being dropped. Warnings and errors stay until you close them, and other messages fade after five seconds. The form's live preview stays quiet. (see spec 0006)
- List filters leave out link fields whose target you can't read, so a Books User sees no Paid From filter on Expenses.
- The Account balances card's default list now leaves out Cash and Bank accounts whose root type is not Asset or Liability.
- Reading the Account balances card now also needs read on Accounting Settings. Every Books role has it today, so no one loses the card.
- On a locked hosted site, an API call that writes fails: an insert or rename answers `SiteLockedError` (HTTP 403), and any other write answers `InReadOnlyMode` (HTTP 503). Check the error type rather than the status, since a 503 here means "read only", not an outage. The desk at /app shows Frappe's own read only banner.
- Saving Accounting Settings is refused when a new or changed Dashboard Accounts row holds a group account or one that isn't Asset or Liability, or when an account is picked twice. Deleting an account that is on the table is refused while it stays there.
- A signed billing update sent to `receive_state` answers 400 only when one of its values is bad, and logs one "Books billing: receive_state refused a bad value" row. Any other failure, such as a database error, now answers 500, rolls back and changes nothing, so the control site can retry it. A pull follows the same split: only a bad value is logged as "get_state sent a bad value". (see spec 0007, lock hardening)

### Fixed
- Two billing updates that reach a hosted site at the same moment are both accepted now, and the newer one wins. Before, one of them could be refused as a bad value, which could leave a paid site locked. Every change to Books Subscription (billing updates, pulls, a started checkout, the trial start and `apply_state`) now waits its turn on a row lock, taken only after billing has answered. (see spec 0007, lock hardening)
- The first save refused after a site turns read only, as at midnight, now shows the current phase's text right away, and the banner updates with it. /books fetches a fresh status before it words the notice; refusals that arrive together share one fetch, and if it fails the notice uses the status it already had.
- During Frappe's own maintenance mode (with `allow_reads_during_maintenance` on), a refused save now says "This site is being updated and is read only for a few minutes. Try again shortly." instead of asking you to subscribe.
- A time zone in System Settings that the server doesn't know no longer makes the subscription clock, the banner or the lock check fail: they count days in UTC instead. Frappe itself still fails signed in requests with that zone, so you'll want to fix the setting.
- A pull that fails for a reason other than an unreachable billing site (a deadlock, say) no longer keeps the Subscription page on Retry until the pull window ends. The next refresh pulls again.

### Security
- A checkout link from the billing control site must start with `https://`, the same rule `books_control_url` already follows (`http://` is allowed only with `developer_mode` on). Any other link shows "Couldn't reach billing. Try again.", leaves the payment pending note off, and writes one Error Log row: "Books billing: create_checkout sent a link that isn't https", or "sent no checkout link" when the answer has none. (see spec 0007, lock hardening)
